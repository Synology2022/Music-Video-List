"Make It Big" is the second studio album by British pop duo Wham!, released back in 1984. Composed of George Michael and Andrew Ridgeley, Wham! became one of the most celebrated pop acts of the 1980s, and this album was instrumental in catapulting them to global stardom. So, what better than to take this music into the modern Atmos era with an exclusive edition from Sony Music made available to the Super Deluxe Edition Shop. High resolution spatial enthusiasts can now enjoy the album on this region-free Blu-ray mixed in Dolby Atmos by Chris Porter along with a hi-res 96kHz / 24-bit stereo version and 21 bonus tracks, 11 which are also in Atmos and stereo too.
